\caption{
\textbf{Cross-dataset zero-shot generalization of DiffCRISP across sequencing batches.}
\textbf{a,} Schematic of the cross-dataset zero-shot prediction setting. DiffCRISP is trained using control and perturbed cells from the NeurIPS dataset and is subsequently evaluated on PBMC-bench cells collected with different sequencing technologies, including both seen and unseen drug perturbations.
\textbf{b,} UMAP visualization of PBMC-bench cells in the original gene-expression space and the latent representation learned by DiffCRISP. Cells are colored according to cell type.
\textbf{c,} Directional prediction accuracy for seen-drug perturbations in NK cells across six sequencing batches. Box plots compare DiffCRISP and CRISP; center lines indicate medians, boxes span the interquartile range (IQR), and whiskers extend to values within $1.5\times$ IQR. For each batch, $m$ denotes the number of available control cells.
\textbf{d,} Prediction accuracy across B cells, NK cells, and CD4$^{+}$ T cells for seen and unseen drug perturbations. Bars show the mean directional accuracy for DiffCRISP and CRISP, with error bars indicating 95\% confidence intervals. For each cell type, $n$ denotes the number of evaluated perturbation conditions.
\textbf{e,} Directional prediction accuracy for unseen-drug perturbations in NK cells across the six sequencing batches. Box-plot elements are defined as in panel \textbf{c}, and $m$ denotes the number of control cells available in each batch.
\textbf{f,} Gene-level comparison of perturbation responses for Dactolisib-treated NK cells measured with 10x Chromium (v3). Scatter plots compare reference log fold changes (logFC) from NeurIPS with predictions transferred to PBMC-bench by DiffCRISP and CRISP. Dark blue points denote the top 50 differentially expressed (DE) genes and light blue points denote the remaining genes. Dashed lines indicate zero logFC, and $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$ and Spearman correlation (Sp) are reported for the top 50 DE genes.

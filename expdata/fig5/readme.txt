\textbf{Ablation analyses and zero-shot generalization of DiffCRISP.}
\textbf{a,} Ablation of molecular drug representations on the NeurIPS dataset. FCFP4, RDKit2D and ChemBERTa representations are compared using $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$, Sinkhorn distance and $R^2$ calculated on DE genes.
\textbf{b,} Component ablation on the NeurIPS dataset across three data splits. The full DiffCRISP model is compared with variants trained without the $L_1$ reconstruction loss or without classifier-free guidance (CFG), using $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$ on the top 50 DE genes.
\textbf{c,} Schematic of the combined unseen-cell-type and unseen-drug prediction setting, in which drug--cell perturbation combinations are held out from perturbed training data while control cells from the target cell type remain available to provide cellular context.
\textbf{d,} Comparison of DiffCRISP and CRISP for unseen-drug prediction in MCF7 cells on SciPlex3. Bars show mean $R^2$, $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$ and Sinkhorn distance on DE genes across 36 unseen drug--dose perturbation conditions, with individual points representing paired conditions and error bars indicating 95\% confidence intervals. $P$ values were obtained using paired one-sided $t$-tests.
\textbf{e,} Schematic of the stricter zero-shot setting, in which the target cell type is excluded from both control and perturbed training populations and the tested drugs are also unseen during training; control cells from the held-out cell type are supplied only at inference.
\textbf{f,} Zero-shot prediction performance across A549, K562 and MCF7 cells in SciPlex3. The upper panel shows the MMD between observed control and treated populations on the top 50 DE genes, reflecting the magnitude of the observed perturbation response. The lower panel compares $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$ between DiffCRISP and CRISP across held-out drug--dose conditions; annotations indicate the relative improvement of DiffCRISP where applicable.
\textbf{g,} Dose-dependent zero-shot prediction performance across held-out drug--cell combinations. DiffCRISP and CRISP are compared using mean $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$ across four dosage levels, with error bars indicating 95\% confidence intervals. The grey dashed curve shows the corresponding MMD between observed control and treated populations on the top 50 DE genes, using the right axis as a measure of perturbation magnitude.

\caption{
\textbf{Pathway-level prioritization of breast cancer therapeutics using DiffCRISP.}
\textbf{a,} Representative breast cancer-related pathway responses predicted for Toremifene, Fulvestrant, and Lapatinib in MCF7 cells. Heatmap values indicate normalized enrichment scores (NES), with positive and negative values denoting pathway activation and suppression, respectively. Numbers in parentheses indicate the number of drugs among the three showing the corresponding pathway response.
\textbf{b,} Ranking of candidate compounds according to the signed pathway score derived from the predefined breast cancer-related pathway signature. Higher scores indicate stronger agreement between the predicted transcriptional response and the expected pathway-level therapeutic pattern.
\textbf{c,} Precision of breast cancer drug prioritization among the top-ranked candidate compounds. Precision is reported for different values of $K$, and the dashed horizontal line denotes the random-selection baseline.
\textbf{d,} Pathway enrichment profile predicted for Fulvestrant-treated MCF7 cells. The horizontal position indicates the normalized enrichment score, whereas bubble size and color reflect pathway significance as quantified by $\log_{10}(1/\mathrm{FDR})$. Positive and negative NES values represent enriched and suppressed pathways, respectively.
\textbf{e,} Representative GSEA enrichment profiles for the Breast Cancer Pathway and Cell Cycle following Fulvestrant treatment. Enrichment curves show the running enrichment score across ranked genes, with the corresponding NES, nominal $P$ value, and false discovery rate (FDR) reported for each pathway.

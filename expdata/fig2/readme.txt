\caption{
\textbf{Benchmarking DiffCRISP for unseen perturbation prediction.}
\textbf{a,} Schematic of the unseen cell-type perturbation prediction task. Models are trained using observed control and perturbed cells and are evaluated by predicting responses to unseen drug perturbations in held-out cell types.
\textbf{b,} Comparison of perturbation prediction performance on the NeurIPS dataset. Box plots summarize nine evaluations for each method using $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$, $R^2$, and Sinkhorn distance calculated over the top 50 differentially expressed (DE) genes; higher $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$ and $R^2$, and lower Sinkhorn distance, indicate better performance.
\textbf{c,} Performance of DiffCRISP and baseline methods across three data splits of the NeurIPS and SciPlex3 datasets. Heatmaps report $R^2$ and $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$ scores on the top 50 DE genes for each method and split.
\textbf{d,} Gene-level agreement between predicted and observed perturbation responses for Palbociclib and Idelalisib. Scatter plots compare predicted and observed log fold changes (logFC) for DiffCRISP, CRISP, and ChemCPA. Dark blue points denote the top 50 DE genes, light blue points denote the remaining genes, and dashed lines indicate perfect agreement. $\mathrm{Pr}_{\Delta}$ and Spearman correlation (Sp) are reported for the top 50 DE genes.
\textbf{e,} Directional prediction accuracy on the top 50 DE genes across six held-out cell types. For each cell type, $n$ denotes the number of evaluated cell type--drug perturbation combinations and $m$ denotes the number of control cells. Box centers indicate medians, boxes span the interquartile range (IQR), and whiskers extend to values within $1.5\times$ IQR.
\textbf{f,} Single-cell expression distributions of ZFAS1 and SNHG6 in Dactolisib-treated CD4$^{+}$ T cells, comparing control cells, observed treated cells, and predictions from CRISP and DiffCRISP. Cells with expression values beyond 2 s.d. from the corresponding observed-group mean were excluded where applicable.

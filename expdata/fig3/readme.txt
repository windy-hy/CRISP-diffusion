\caption{
\textbf{Distribution recovery and sampling variability of DiffCRISP.}
\textbf{a,} Comparison of population-level distribution recovery between CRISP and DiffCRISP across three data splits of the NeurIPS dataset. Bars show the mean $\pm$ SEM across perturbation conditions for Sinkhorn distance, centered Sinkhorn distance, and variance log-MAE, with lower values indicating better recovery.
\textbf{b,} Perturbation-level paired comparisons of the three distribution metrics after $\log(1+x)$ transformation. Dashed diagonals indicate equal performance between the two models. Win rates denote the proportion of perturbation conditions for which DiffCRISP achieves lower errors than CRISP, and $P$ values were calculated using two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.
\textbf{c--d,} Sampling variability across multiple K562 drug perturbations in the SciPlex3 dataset.
\textbf{c,} Between-seed variation measured as the mean pairwise Sinkhorn distance among populations generated with ten independent sampling seeds for each perturbation condition. Connected points represent the same perturbation condition evaluated with CRISP and DiffCRISP, and horizontal bars indicate medians.
\textbf{d,} Relative sampling variation, defined as the ratio between variation among generated populations and the corresponding variation obtained by repeatedly resampling observed treated cells. Values closer to 1 indicate better agreement with the intrinsic sampling variability of the observed population. Extreme perturbation conditions outside the 2.5th--97.5th percentile range were excluded from panels \textbf{c} and \textbf{d} for visualization.
\textbf{e,} Pairwise Sinkhorn distances among populations generated with ten sampling seeds for K562 cells treated with Cerdulatinib at dose 1.0. Heatmaps are calculated using all genes, with diagonal entries omitted and mean off-diagonal distances shown above each heatmap. Color scales are displayed separately for CRISP and DiffCRISP.

a–b, Comparison of distribution recovery between DiffCRISP and CRISP on the NeurIPS dataset.** **a**, Mean Sinkhorn distance, centered Sinkhorn distance, and variance log-MAE across the three data splits; error bars indicate SEM across perturbation conditions. **b**, Paired comparison of the corresponding distribution metrics for individual perturbation conditions, shown after log(1+x) transformation. The dashed diagonal indicates equal performance between the two models. Win rates denote the proportion of perturbations for which DiffCRISP achieves a lower error than CRISP, with (P) values obtained using two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.
